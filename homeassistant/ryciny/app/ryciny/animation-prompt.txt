Use case: reference-based illustration sequence.
Asset type: one atlas of six stages of a complete stop-motion activity for an ambient display.
Input image 1 is the reference identity, proportions, coat colors and engraving style of {subject}.
Create a single transparent PNG sprite sheet, landscape 1536 by 1024 pixels, with EXACTLY THREE equal columns and TWO equal rows (six square 512x512 cells), no gutters, no labels, no borders, no grid lines, no text.
Each cell contains a fresh drawing of the same recognizable subject, progressing through anticipation, action and release. The timeline must describe an activity, not merely six viewing angles. Viewpoint and contour changes may support the action. Preserve identity, anatomy, mechanical structure, restrained colors and fine cross-hatched copperplate engraving. Keep comparable visual size and a shared baseline. Fit each complete drawing within 420x420 pixels, with at least 46 pixels of transparent margin on every side of its cell. No clipped or overlapping subjects. The cutout has NO paper, shadow, scenery or texture behind it: the product preserves its existing stationary paper as a separate layer.
{motion}
Keep a consistent viewing side and the same arrangement of identifying parts throughout all six stages. Never mirror the subject, swap left and right, add or remove limbs or machine parts, or turn the sequence into a camera orbit. Each new pose must follow naturally from the previous stage of the activity. Plan anticipation, peak action and settling before drawing all six cells. Every generated cell is used in order; the workflow does not rely on a person rejecting individual frames.
These are discrete artistic drawings played at about six frames per second in a short burst. Allow the whole posture to change coherently through one complete action, then settle toward the starting pose. No motion blur, interpolation, physical card flips, flying paper or video effects.
